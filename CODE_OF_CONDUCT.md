# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Zyntalic respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and cultural context.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and moderation gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, sexualized attention, intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or the repository's private security/contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently ban participants when behavior threatens a safe and productive community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project. It is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
